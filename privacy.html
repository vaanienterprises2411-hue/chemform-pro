Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026  ·  Effective date: May 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Vaani Enterprises ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use ChemForm Pro — available at chemformpro.in and on the Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information when you register and use ChemForm Pro:

  • Account information: your name, email address or phone number, city, and country — provided during registration
  • Industry information: the industry you select at signup, used to personalise your formula recommendations
  • Usage data: number of AI optimizations, formula views, and feature usage per month — used to enforce plan limits
  • Formula requests: product name, application, and requirements you submit for custom formulation requests
  • Payment information: we do not store card details — payments processed by Razorpay (India) and Stripe (international)
  • Device and log data: device type, OS, IP address, and app usage logs for analytics and security

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Provide and operate the ChemForm Pro service
  • Personalise formula recommendations based on your industry
  • Enforce subscription plan limits (AI credits, process views, etc.)
  • Process payments for subscriptions and custom formula requests
  • Respond to custom formulation requests within 24 hours
  • Send important service updates and account notifications
  • Improve the app based on usage patterns
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to any third party.

3. Advertising

ChemForm Pro displays advertisements to Free plan users using Google AdMob. Google may use your device advertising ID to show relevant ads. You can opt out in your device settings under Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalisation.

Paid plan users (Starter, Pro, Enterprise) see no advertisements.

We may display sponsored ingredient cards from chemical suppliers, clearly labelled as "Sponsored" or "Featured Supplier".

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (hosted on AWS) with industry-standard encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest. OTP-based authentication means we never store passwords.

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with:

  • Supabase — database and authentication
  • Razorpay / Stripe — payment processing
  • Google AdMob — advertising (free users only)
  • Anthropic — AI optimization (formula data sent to Claude API; not stored for training)
  • Vercel — web hosting

6. Custom Formula Requests

Details you provide in custom formulation requests are used solely to prepare and deliver your formulation. Formulations may be anonymously added to the ChemForm Pro library — no personally identifiable information is published.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data, opt out of marketing, and export your data. Email info@chemformpro.in and we will respond within 7 business days.

8. Data Retention

Account data is retained while your account is active. Deleted accounts are purged within 30 days. Anonymised analytics may be retained for up to 2 years.

9. Children's Privacy

ChemForm Pro is intended for professional use by adults (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance. Registered users will be notified by email of significant changes.
