Dishwash Liquid Formulation: Ingredients, Process & Plant Setup

Dishwash liquid is a mild, high-foam surfactant gel designed for grease removal and skin compatibility. It is close to neutral pH and thickened with salt.

Manufacturing process

  1. Neutralise LABSA in water under stirring.
  2. Add SLES and CAPB slowly to avoid foam.
  3. Adjust pH to near neutral.
  4. Build viscosity with salt; add fragrance, colour and preservative.
  5. De-aerate and fill.

Machinery required

  • SS mixing tank with low-shear agitator
  • Dosing and weighing
  • Filling machine

Quality control tests

  • Active matter
  • pH
  • Viscosity
  • Foam and grease-cutting (plate count) test

Frequently asked questions

Why add CAPB to dishwash liquid?

It boosts and stabilises foam and makes the product milder on hands.

What pH should dishwash liquid be?

Close to neutral so it is gentle on skin.
