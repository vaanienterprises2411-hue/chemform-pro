Liquid Detergent Formulation: Ingredients, Process & Plant Setup

Liquid laundry detergent is a blend of anionic and nonionic surfactants with builders and performance additives. The core step is neutralising LABSA to form the sulfonate, then building viscosity and fragrance.

Liquid Detergent ingredients and their role

IngredientFunction
LABSA (linear alkyl benzene sulfonic acid)Main anionic surfactant after neutralisation
Caustic soda / soda ashNeutralises LABSA
SLESFoam and detergency
Nonionic surfactant (fatty alcohol ethoxylate)Oily soil removal, cold-water performance
Builder (sodium citrate / STPP)Softens hard water
CMC / anti-redeposition polymerStops soil settling back on fabric
Optical brightenerWhiteness
Salt, perfume, colour, preservativeViscosity, fragrance, appearance, shelf life

Manufacturing process

  1. Charge water and neutralising alkali; add LABSA slowly under stirring and control temperature.
  2. Check pH, then add SLES and nonionic.
  3. Add builder, anti-redeposition polymer and optical brightener.
  4. Adjust viscosity with salt; add perfume, colour and preservative.
  5. Rest to de-aerate, then fill.

Machinery required

  • SS stirred tank with slow agitator
  • Weighing and dosing
  • Transfer pump and filter
  • Bottle filling and capping

Quality control tests

  • Active matter
  • pH
  • Viscosity
  • Foam height
  • Stability (heat and cold)

Frequently asked questions

Why does liquid detergent become thin?

Usually wrong salt level (too much salt drops viscosity past the peak) or a pH drift; build a salt curve for your surfactant system.

Can I make detergent without LABSA?

Yes, SLES and nonionic systems are used, but LABSA is the most cost-effective detergency base.
