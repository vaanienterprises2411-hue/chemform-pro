Phenyl (White & Black) Formulation: Ingredients, Process & Plant Setup

Phenyl is a phenolic disinfectant floor cleaner. Black phenyl is a concentrate of tar acid oils emulsified with a rosin soap; white phenyl is a milky emulsion based on pine oil and phenolic compounds. Indian specification is IS 1061 with Rideal-Walker (RW) coefficient as the key strength measure.

Phenyl (White & Black) ingredients and their role

Manufacturing process

  1. Black: saponify rosin with caustic soda to form rosin soap.
  2. Black: blend in tar acid oil under stirring to form a clear concentrate.
  3. White: mix pine oil, phenolic active and emulsifier into an oil phase.
  4. White: add the oil phase to water under agitation to form a stable milky emulsion.
  5. Check RW coefficient and stability; fill in bottles or cans.

Machinery required

  • MS / SS kettle with stirrer (saponification)
  • Mixing tank with agitator
  • Storage tanks
  • Filling and capping machine

Quality control tests

  • Rideal-Walker coefficient
  • Emulsion stability on dilution
  • Appearance and odour
  • pH

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between white and black phenyl?

Black phenyl is a dark concentrate of tar acids with rosin soap; white phenyl is a milky pine oil/phenolic emulsion. Both are diluted before use.

What does RW coefficient mean on phenyl?

It compares the germicidal strength of the product with pure phenol; a higher RW value means a stronger disinfectant.
