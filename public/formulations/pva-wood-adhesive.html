PVA Wood Adhesive (White Glue) Formulation: Ingredients, Process & Plant Setup

White wood glue is a polyvinyl acetate (PVAc) homopolymer emulsion stabilised with polyvinyl alcohol (PVOH). It can be made by polymerising vinyl acetate monomer in-house, or by compounding a bought-in PVAc emulsion with plasticiser, filler and additives.

PVA Wood Adhesive (White Glue) ingredients and their role

IngredientFunction
Vinyl acetate monomer (VAM)Monomer for the PVAc polymer (in-house route)
Polyvinyl alcohol (PVOH)Protective colloid; gives tack, viscosity and stability
Initiator (persulfate / redox system)Starts emulsion polymerisation
Plasticiser (non-phthalate, e.g. dibenzoate)Lowers film-forming temperature, improves flexibility
Filler (calcium carbonate / china clay)Economy grades and gap filling
Biocide (BIT / CMIT-MIT)In-can preservation
DefoamerControls foam in production and use

Manufacturing process

  1. Dissolve PVOH in hot water in the jacketed reactor.
  2. Add part of the VAM and initiator; start the reaction under reflux.
  3. Feed the remaining monomer and initiator gradually while controlling temperature.
  4. Hold to finish conversion, then cool and strip residual monomer.
  5. Compound with plasticiser, filler, biocide and defoamer; filter and pack.

Machinery required

  • Jacketed SS reactor with reflux condenser and agitator
  • Monomer and initiator dosing tanks
  • Cooling water / chiller
  • Blending tank and filter
  • Filling line (jars, buckets, drums)

Quality control tests

  • Solids content
  • Brookfield viscosity
  • pH
  • Residual monomer
  • Bond strength / wood failure test
  • Minimum film-forming temperature (MFFT)

Frequently asked questions

Can I make wood glue without a reactor?

Yes, by buying PVAc emulsion and compounding it with plasticiser, filler and additives in a stirred tank. Margins are lower than the polymerisation route.

Why is PVOH used in white glue?

It stabilises the emulsion particles and gives the glue its tack and set speed on wood.
