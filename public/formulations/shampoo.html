Shampoo Formulation: Ingredients, Process & Plant Setup

A conditioning shampoo is a surfactant system (primary anionic plus amphoteric) with a conditioning polymer, thickener, pearl and preservative, adjusted to a skin-friendly pH.

Shampoo ingredients and their role

IngredientFunction
Sodium laureth sulfate (SLES)Primary cleansing surfactant
Cocamidopropyl betaine (CAPB)Mildness and foam stability
Thickener (cocamide MEA/DEA or polymer) as a pre-mixViscosity; pre-dispersed to avoid lumps
Polyquaternium-10 / guar hydroxypropyltrimonium chlorideConditioning and wet combability
Glycol distearatePearl effect
Disodium EDTAChelation, stability
Preservative, citric acid, salt, fragranceShelf life, pH, viscosity, scent

Manufacturing process

  1. Hydrate the conditioning polymer in water first.
  2. Prepare the thickener / pearl pre-mix separately (warm if required).
  3. Add SLES, then CAPB, under gentle stirring.
  4. Add the pre-mix, EDTA and preservative.
  5. Adjust pH with citric acid, viscosity with salt, then fragrance; fill.

Machinery required

  • SS 316 jacketed mixing vessel
  • Pre-mix vessel
  • Homogeniser (optional)
  • Filling and capping line

Quality control tests

  • pH
  • Viscosity
  • Foam
  • Microbial count
  • Stability (heat, cold, light)

Frequently asked questions

Why is my shampoo lumpy?

The thickener or conditioning polymer was added dry into the surfactant; disperse it as a pre-mix first.

What pH should shampoo be?

Mildly acidic, close to the skin and hair's natural pH.
