Tile Adhesive Formulation: Ingredients, Process & Plant Setup

Tile adhesive is a polymer-modified cementitious dry mix that replaces sand-cement mortar for fixing ceramic, vitrified and stone tiles. Classes C1 (normal) and C2 (improved) under IS 15477 / EN 12004 differ mainly in polymer and cellulose ether dosage, which drives adhesion, open time and slip resistance.

Tile Adhesive ingredients and their role

IngredientFunction
OPC 53 grade or white cementHydraulic binder that develops the bond strength
Graded silica sand (fine, dried)Aggregate; particle size controls trowel feel and bed thickness
Calcium carbonate / calcite powderFiller for packing density and cost control
Redispersible polymer powder (VAE RDP)Flexibility and adhesion to dense vitrified tiles; key C1 vs C2 lever
HPMC / MHEC cellulose etherWater retention, open time, workability
Starch etherAnti-slip / anti-sag on walls
Calcium formateAccelerator for early strength and cold weather
Defoamer / PP fibre (optional)Air control and crack resistance

Manufacturing process

  1. Dry the sand to below 0.5% moisture and sieve it into the required fractions.
  2. Weigh cement, sand and filler; pre-blend the small additives (RDP, cellulose ether, starch ether, formate) separately.
  3. Charge the mixer with coarse materials first, then the additive pre-blend.
  4. Mix in a plough-share or ribbon mixer until uniform (typically a few minutes per batch).
  5. Pack in 20 or 25 kg valve bags and run batch QC before dispatch.

Machinery required

  • Sand dryer and vibrating sieve
  • Plough-share or ribbon mixer
  • Weighing hoppers / platform scales
  • Valve bag packing machine

Quality control tests

  • Tensile adhesion strength (EN 1348): dry, water immersion, heat ageing, freeze–thaw
  • Open time (EN 1346)
  • Slip (EN 1308)
  • Pot life and consistency

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between C1 and C2 tile adhesive?

C2 must meet higher tensile adhesion (1 N/mm² vs 0.5 N/mm²) after all conditioning, which usually needs more redispersible polymer powder and cellulose ether.

Can I use white cement instead of grey?

Yes. White cement is used for light-coloured stone and glass mosaic so the adhesive does not stain or show through.
